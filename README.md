\# AI Employee Workforce Platform



AI-powered workforce management platform built with FastAPI, React, SQLAlchemy, SQLite and Gemini AI.



\## 🚀 Features



\- Employee Management

\- Task Management

\- Performance Management

\- Dashboard Analytics

\- AI Workforce Assistant

\- AI Workforce Insights

\- Smart Task Assignment

\- Employee Risk Prediction

\- Performance Analytics

\- Task Workload Analytics

\- Task Status Analytics

\- Responsive UI



\## 🤖 AI Features



\### AI Workforce Assistant

Allows users to ask questions about:

\- Employee performance

\- Employee workload

\- Assigned tasks

\- Workforce information



\### AI Workforce Insights

Analyzes:

\- Workforce performance

\- Employee workload

\- Organization risk

\- AI recommendations



\### Smart Task Assignment

Recommends the most suitable employee based on:

\- Current workload

\- Performance score

\- Completed tasks



\### Employee Risk Prediction

Analyzes employee workload and performance to identify:

\- Low risk

\- Medium risk

\- High risk
